# WordWise: Teacher guide

For class teachers of Std 5. Allow about 45 minutes to read this once and set up your first class. Part 1 to 4 are everything you need for daily use. Part 5 is only for teachers running the research study.

> **Your address:** replace `YOUR-APP` below with your school's WordWise address (for example `avishkar-vocab-app.vercel.app`).

---

## Part 1. Join your school and create your class

### 1.1 Join (once)
1. Open the **teacher link** your principal sent on WhatsApp (`https://YOUR-APP/t/ABCDEFGH`). Or open `https://YOUR-APP/teacher/join` and type the school code (like `ABCD-EFGH`).
2. Type your **name, email and a password** (8 or more characters). Tap **Join school**.
3. Write your email and password somewhere safe. If you forget the password, ask your principal for a **reset link** (see 1.4).

### 1.2 Log in later
Open `https://YOUR-APP/teacher` and log in with your email and password. The first screen is **Home**. It shows **What to do next**, a short checklist that ticks itself as you finish each step.

### 1.3 Create your class
1. On Home tap **Create your class**.
2. Pick the **standard** (for example 5) and the **division** (for example B).
3. Tap **Create class**. WordWise gives the class a 6-letter **class code** (for example `K7Q2MX`).

A school can have many classes, but one class slot (standard + division) can be used only once.

### 1.4 Forgot your password
Ask your principal. They tap **Reset password** next to your name and send you a link on WhatsApp (`https://YOUR-APP/r/ABCDEFGH`). It works once and ends in 24 hours.

## Part 2. Add and manage children

### 2.1 Add children
1. Open your class and tap **Add students**.
2. **Paste** your class list, one child per line, like `1, Riya` or `2 Aarav`. You can copy it straight from WhatsApp, Excel or Google Sheets. Or **upload a CSV file**.
3. Only **first names** are kept. WordWise does not store surnames, phone numbers or photos.
4. Check the preview. Tick **Parents have given consent**. Tap **Save**.

Each child gets a **roll number**, an **animal avatar** and a **4-digit PIN**. Two children in the same class never get the same PIN.

### 2.2 Print login cards
1. Tap **Print login cards** (the **Cards** page).
2. Print it (Ctrl+P). Cut the cards and hand each child their own. Each card has the name, PIN and a QR code.
3. Keep a spare copy locked in your cupboard.

### 2.3 Share the class link with parents
On the **Share** screen tap **Send on WhatsApp**. The message contains the class link `https://YOUR-APP/j/K7Q2MX`. A parent opens it on the child's phone, taps the child's name and types the PIN.

You can also pin the **QR code** on the class board.

### 2.4 The Roster
**Roster** lists every child with their status. From here you can:

| What you want | What to do |
|---|---|
| A child forgot the PIN | Tap **Reset PIN** next to the name, then give the child the new PIN. This also signs the child out of other phones. |
| A child is locked out (typed the wrong PIN too many times) | The row shows **locked**. Wait for it to unlock, or tap **Reset PIN**. |
| Rename a child, fix a roll number | Tap the child, edit, save. |
| A child left the class | Turn the child **off** (deactivate). Their history stays, but they no longer appear on the login screen. |
| A new child joins | **Add students** again, or approve their request (2.5). |

### 2.5 Approve or remove children who ask to join
A child (or parent) can open the class link and tap **I'm not on the list** to send a **join request** with a first name. Nobody gets in automatically.

1. Open **Roster**. If requests are waiting you see **Waiting to join (n)**.
2. Tap **Approve** to add the child (they get a PIN), or **Remove** to turn the request down.
3. Give the child their PIN. The child's own phone also shows the result.

This is how you stop strangers or the wrong children entering your class.

### 2.6 Children sharing a phone
If two children use the same phone, the app shows **Who's playing?**. To open **another** child's profile, or remove one from the phone, the app asks for **that child's PIN**. A child cannot play as, or delete, a classmate without knowing the PIN.

## Part 3. Every day

### 3.1 Today dashboard
Open your class, then **Dashboard** (title: **Today**). It shows who has joined, who has played today, who finished all five words, and who has not started. Use it for a 2-minute check at the start of the day.

### 3.2 What children do each day
Five words a day. For each word the child hears it, spells it, checks the meaning, chooses its part of speech (noun, verb, adjective or adverb) and writes a sentence. They earn stars and XP, build a streak and see their level. Details are in the Student guide.

### 3.3 Choose or change today's words
Open **Words**.
- By default WordWise picks five words for you.
- To change a day, search the **word picker** (filter by difficulty, part of speech or tag), choose words, tap **Save day**.
- To use your own word, tap **Custom word**, type the word, meaning, difficulty and example sentence, tap **Add word**.
- Tap **Reset to auto** to go back to the automatic words. Changed days are marked **Changed**, your own words **Custom**.

### 3.4 Leaderboard
Open **Leaderboard** to see **This week** and **All time**. Switch **Show leaderboard to students** off if you do not want children to see rankings. Some classes prefer this.

### 3.5 Class analytics
Open **Analytics** and pick a date range (**From** and **To**). The page shows how the class is doing and the **Hardest words**, the words most children got wrong. Revise those on the board.

### 3.6 One child's detail
Tap any child to see:
- **Strength** and **Needs support**
- **Spelling errors**: the exact wrong spellings they typed
- **Sentence writing** with marks for **word use, grammar, relevance and length**, plus **Common sentence issues**
- **Recent sessions**, **streak**, and the child's sentences

Use this for parent meetings and to pick children for extra help.

### 3.7 Progress record (printable)
Open **Progress** for the class. It lists each child's days active, words completed, accuracy and last active day, with a **Daily vocabulary assignment record**: one row per child, one column per day. Tap **Print record** to print it or save it as PDF.

## Part 4. Tips and fixes

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| The link opens inside WhatsApp and looks odd | Tap the three dots, choose **Open in Chrome**. |
| A child sees "No internet. Showing saved info." | The app keeps working for words already loaded. Reconnect and it syncs. |
| A phone shows "A new version is ready" | Tap **Refresh**. It happens after an update. |
| A child pressed **Log out** and forgot the PIN | **Reset PIN** on the Roster. |
| The app should work like a normal app | On the child's phone choose **Add to Home screen**. The app also offers this itself. |
| A child cannot find their name | They are not in the class yet, deactivated, or in another class. Check the Roster. |

Privacy in one line: children use first names and a PIN only. Parents' consent is ticked when you add the children, and nothing identifies a child outside the school.

## Part 5. Running the research study (optional)

This part is for teachers taking part in the Aavishkar study. The default design is **two weeks: 10 school days, 5 new words a day, 50 words**, with a pre-test, a post-test and an optional comparison class.

### 5.1 Create the study
1. Open **Study** (Studies). Tap **Create study**.
2. Give it a name, a **start date** (a school day) and **School days** (default **10**).
3. Add your class as **Intervention** (uses WordWise every day). Optionally add another class as **Comparison** (takes the tests but does not use the app daily). A class can be in only one study at a time.

### 5.2 Consent and study codes
1. Open **Students and consent**.
2. Set each child's consent: **given**, **declined**, **withdrawn**, or **excluded** (for example a long absence). Only children with consent **given** are in the research data. The others can still use the app.
3. Every child gets an anonymous code: **S01, S02...** for the intervention class and **C01, C02...** for the comparison class. Tap **Print code sheet** and keep it in the school file. It is never exported.

### 5.3 The word plan
Open **Words plan**. You see the 10 days with 5 words each, and how many nouns, verbs, adjectives and adverbs. The first 10 days teach exactly the 50 words used in the tests, so no child is tested on a word they never met.
- Add **Holiday** dates. Weekends and holidays are skipped automatically.
- You may swap words on a day, but keep the 50 test words in the plan, or the post-test will include unseen words.
- Tap **Print the list** for a copy.

### 5.4 Pre-test and post-test
1. Open the class, then **Tests**. Create the **pre-test** (Form A) and set **Minutes** (default 25). Choose **In the app** or **On paper**.
2. On test day tap **Open test**. Write the 4-letter **Test code** on the board.
3. Children tap **Test**, type the code and answer one question per screen. They do **not** see marks.
4. Watch who has started and submitted. Tap **Close test** when everyone is done.
5. Do the **post-test** (Form B) the same way after the 10 days.
6. **On paper:** tap **Print form A** or **Print form B**, then **Save paper marks** for each child. Use the **Marking scheme**.
7. Review sentence answers (Part C) and change a mark if needed. Auto marks and your final marks are both kept.

Total is out of **30**: Part A meanings (10), Part B parts of speech (10), Part C own sentences (10).

### 5.5 During the study
- **Progress record** (3.7) shows participation per day.
- **Sentence check** (Authenticity) lists sentences that look copied, for example the same frame reused, or the same sentence as a classmate. They are **hints, not accusations**: mark each **Own work** or **Not own work**. Children never see this.
- **Weekly observation:** in **Instruments** pick the week and tap Yes or No for six points per child (uses the app regularly, understands meanings, identifies parts of speech, writes own sentences, takes part, revises earlier words).
- **Student questionnaire:** at the end open the questionnaire so children answer five short questions in the app. You can also type paper answers. Tap **Close questionnaire** when done.
- Children also do a **Recall challenge** each day and have a **My Word Bank** (see the Student guide).

### 5.6 Results and export
Open **Results**:
- Tiles: children analysed, mean pre, mean post, mean gain, % improvement and the p-value.
- Charts: pre vs post by skill, each child pre to post, gains.
- **Statistics by skill** and, if you have a comparison class, **Intervention vs comparison** gains.
- **Participation**, **Student feedback**, **Teacher observation** and **Sentence authenticity** summaries.
- **Download research pack** (CSV files with study codes only, never names) and **Printable report** (**Print or save as PDF**).

A child is included only if consent is **given**, not excluded, and both tests are complete.

### 5.7 If a parent withdraws a child
Open **Students and consent**, set the child to **withdrawn**, then use **Delete research data** for that child. Their research rows are removed. Their normal app use stays so your class history is not broken.

### 5.8 Close the study
When everything is done, set the study status to **closed**.
