# WordWise training documents

| Guide | For | Read time |
|---|---|---|
| [PRINCIPAL_GUIDE.md](PRINCIPAL_GUIDE.md) | Principal or school coordinator: set up the school, invite teachers, monitor classes, settings | 20 min |
| [TEACHER_GUIDE.md](TEACHER_GUIDE.md) | Class teachers: classes, children, PINs, daily use, analytics, and the two-week research study | 45 min |
| [STUDENT_GUIDE.md](STUDENT_GUIDE.md) | Std 5 children and the grown-up helping them | 10 min |

Suggested training session (about 90 minutes): principal sets up the school (15 min), every teacher joins and creates a class together (30 min), teachers add their children and print cards (20 min), one teacher shows a child a full day of five words (15 min), questions (10 min).

Replace `YOUR-APP` in the guides with your school's address. These guides describe the screens as they are today; if a button is renamed, update the guide.
