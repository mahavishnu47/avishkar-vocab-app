# WordWise: Principal / school coordinator guide

For the person who sets up the school and keeps an eye on it. Allow about 20 minutes.

> **Your address:** replace `YOUR-APP` with your school's WordWise address.

---

## 1. Set up your school (once)

1. WordWise sends you a **setup link** on WhatsApp: `https://YOUR-APP/a/ABCDEFGH`. It works **once** and lasts **14 days**.
2. Open it. You see **Set up your school**. Type your **name, email and a password** (8 or more characters). Tap **Create my account**.
3. You land on the school dashboard with a **Getting started** checklist. It ticks itself.

Lost the setup link, or the 14 days passed? Ask WordWise for a new one.

You are a **school admin**. You can see every class and teacher in your school. You cannot see another school's data.

---

## 2. Invite your teachers

1. On the dashboard, under **Invite your teachers**, tap the big green **WhatsApp** button. Or open **Teachers** and tap **Make teacher code**.
2. Send the message to your staff group. It has the **teacher link** (`https://YOUR-APP/t/ABCDEFGH`) and the **school code** (`ABCD-EFGH`).
3. Each teacher joins with their name, email and a password and then creates their own class.

In **Teachers** you can:

| What you want | What to do |
|---|---|
| Make a new invite code | **New code** (the old one stops working) |
| Stop new teachers joining | **Turn off code** |
| A teacher forgot the password | **Reset password**. You get a link, **Works once and ends in 24 hours.** Send it on WhatsApp. |
| Stop a teacher using the app | **Turn off** (and **Turn on** later) |
| Make a second admin | **Make admin**. Take it away with **Remove admin**. |

The school must always keep **at least one admin**. WordWise will not let you remove the last one. You also cannot reset your own password from here: ask WordWise for a new setup link.

---

## 3. Watch your school every day

Open `https://YOUR-APP/school` and log in. The **Progress** page shows:

- **Totals:** classes, teachers, children who joined, children who played today
- **Getting started** checklist for the school
- **Top classes this week** (a friendly leaderboard)
- A table per class: **Teacher**, **Students**, **Joined**, **Played today**, **This week**
- A class showing **Needs a teacher** has no teacher yet

Use it in the morning: if a class shows few children playing today, speak to the class teacher.

---

## 4. Classes

Open **Classes**:
- See every class, its teacher, how many children it has and how many have joined.
- **Change a class's teacher**, for example when a teacher leaves.
- **Archive** a class when the year ends. The history is kept.

---

## 5. Settings: school details, student link and poster

Open **Settings**:
- **School:** the school name and district.
- **Student link:** one link for the whole school. Children open it, pick their class and name and type their PIN. Tap **New link** if the link leaks; the old link then stops working (**Yes, make a new link**).
- **Print poster:** a printable poster with a QR code ("Scan to play WordWise") for the notice board.
- **Recent activity:** a log of important events (for example a PIN reset or a teacher being turned off), with who did it.

---

## 6. What teachers and children do

You do not need to do these, but you should know them.

- **Teachers** create a class, paste their class list, print PIN cards, share the class link, approve join requests, reset PINs, choose words, and read dashboards and analytics. See the Teacher guide.
- **Children** play five words a day: hear, spell, meaning, kind of word, own sentence. They see their own progress. They cannot open or delete a classmate's profile without that child's PIN. See the Student guide.
- **Privacy:** only first names and a PIN are stored. There are no photos, phone numbers or surnames. Research exports use anonymous study codes.

---

## 7. If your school is in the research study

You can see the school's studies and consent counts. Teachers run the tests and read the results. Your jobs:
1. Make sure parents' **consent forms** are returned and the teacher ticks consent for each child.
2. Check the school calendar so teachers add **holidays** in the word plan.
3. Allow the test sessions (pre-test and post-test) on the dates agreed. Each is about 25 minutes.
4. Keep the printed **code sheet** (study code against child) in the school file. It is never uploaded or exported.
5. If a parent withdraws a child, ask the teacher to delete that child's research data.

---

## 8. Quick help

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| A teacher cannot log in | Reset their password (section 2). |
| A teacher left the school | **Turn off** the teacher, then change the class's teacher in **Classes**. |
| Children cannot find their class | Check the class is not archived and that the teacher added the children. |
| The student link was shared publicly | **Settings, New link, Yes, make a new link**. |
| You lost your own password | Ask WordWise for a new setup link. |
| The school is paused | The login says "Your school is paused. Contact WordWise." Contact WordWise. |
