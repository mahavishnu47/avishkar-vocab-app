# WordWise: Student guide

For children in Std 5, and the grown-up who helps them. Read it aloud together.

---

## 1. Get in the first time

You need three things: a phone or tablet, your **PIN card** from your teacher, and a grown-up to help.

1. The grown-up opens the **class link** from your teacher on the phone (it looks like `https://YOUR-APP/j/K7Q2MX`). Or scan the **QR code** on your card.
   - No link? Open `https://YOUR-APP`, tap **I'm a Student** and type the class code from your card.
   - If the link opens inside WhatsApp, tap the three dots and choose **Open in Chrome**.
2. Tap **your name**.
3. Type the **4-digit PIN** from your card on the big number pad.
4. Watch the short welcome (about 30 seconds). Tap **Let's play**.

Keep your card safe. **Never tell your PIN to friends.** Your PIN is the lock on your profile.

### I forgot my PIN
Tap **Forgot PIN**. The app says: ask your teacher for your PIN card. Your teacher will give you a new PIN.

### I am not on the list
Tap **I'm not on the list** and type your first name. Your teacher gets a request and will approve you. When you are approved, the app shows your PIN.

---

## 2. Every day: five new words

Tap **Play**. For each word you will:

1. **Listen.** Tap the speaker to **hear the word**. Tap the slow speaker to hear it slowly.
2. **Spell.** Type the word on the on-screen keyboard. If it is wrong, try again. You can ask for a hint.
3. **Meaning.** Choose what the word means.
4. **Kind of word.** Choose if it is a **noun** (naming word), **verb** (doing word), **adjective** (describing word) or **adverb** (how or when word).
5. **Write a sentence.** Write your own sentence using the word. Use your own idea. If you need help, tap **Help me start**.

At the end you see your **Word marks** and the **XP** (points) you earned today. Five words take only a few minutes. Come back **every school day**.

**Good to know**
- You cannot paste text. The keyboard is part of the app. Write in your own words.
- You can use the speaker on any screen to hear the question read aloud.
- If the internet is slow, the app says **No internet. Showing saved info.** Your work is saved and sent later.

---

## 3. Stars, streaks and levels

- You get **stars** and **XP** for correct work.
- A **streak** counts the days in a row you played. Don't break it!
- Your **level** grows as you earn XP.
- The **leaderboard** (trophy button) shows **This week**. Every day you play gives bonus XP. Your teacher may switch the leaderboard off.

---

## 4. My Progress

Tap the **level** button on the home screen to open **My Progress**. You see:
- **My words learned** with a progress bar
- **Last 14 days**: the days you played
- **My skills**: how well you do spelling, meanings, kinds of words and sentences
- **Words to practise**
- **My last days**

If it says **Play your first day!**, start with today's words and your stars will grow.

---

## 5. Recall challenge and My Word Bank

- **Recall challenge:** before today's words you may get a few quick questions on **yesterday's words** and older words you found hard. Tap the answers. This helps your brain keep the words for a long time.
- **My Word Bank:** tap it to see **every word you have learned** with its meaning, kind of word, your best sentence and your stars. Search a word, filter by noun, verb, adjective or adverb, and tap to hear it.

---

## 6. Tests (only when your teacher says)

Your teacher may give you a **Word Test**.
1. Tap **Test**.
2. Type the **4-letter code** your teacher writes on the board.
3. Answer one question per screen. A bar shows the **time left**. Tap **Check your answers** at the end.
4. You will not see a score on the screen. **Just do your best.**
5. When it says **Well done, you finished!**, put the phone down and wait quietly.

Work alone. Do not look at a friend's screen.

---

## 7. Tell us what you think

Near the end of the study your teacher may open five short questions: **Yes**, **To some extent** or **No**. Tap the speaker if you want a question read aloud. There are no wrong answers. Your answers help your teacher.

---

## 8. Sharing a phone with a brother, sister or friend

When more than one child uses a phone, the app asks **Who's playing?**
- Tap **your own face** to play.
- To open **someone else's** profile, you need **their PIN**. This protects everyone's work.
- If it is not you, tap **Not you?** on the home screen.

When you finish, tap **Log out** on the home screen and confirm **Yes, log out**. Next time you will need your PIN again.

---

## 9. Put WordWise on the home screen

On the child's phone, the app will offer **Put WordWise on your home screen**. Tap it, or choose **Add to Home screen** from the browser menu. Then WordWise opens like any other app.

If you see **A new version is ready.**, tap **Refresh**.

---

## 10. Quick help

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| The sound does not play | Turn the volume up. Tap the speaker again. |
| I typed the wrong PIN many times | The profile locks for a short time. Wait, or ask your teacher to reset the PIN. |
| I cannot find my name | Ask your teacher to check the class list. |
| I lost my card | Ask your teacher for a new PIN. |
| I am not sure what to write in a sentence | Think of something real: your home, school, food, friends. Use the word in a full sentence. |

**For parents:** WordWise keeps only your child's first name and a PIN. There are no ads, no chat and no photos. Your teacher can answer any question.
