# WordWise: getting started (one page)

Replace `YOUR-APP` with your school's app address (for example `shabd-mitra.vercel.app`). You never need an email invite: everything works through links you send on WhatsApp.

## Principals (or school coordinators)

1. WordWise sends you a **setup link** on WhatsApp: `https://YOUR-APP/a/ABCDEFGH`. It works once, for 14 days.
2. Open it. You see "Set up your school". Type your **name, email and a password** (8 or more characters). Tap **Create my account**.
3. On the next screen tap the big green **WhatsApp** button under "Invite your teachers". Send the message to your staff group. It contains the **teacher link** (`https://YOUR-APP/t/ABCDEFGH`) and the school code (`ABCD-EFGH`).
4. Each morning open `https://YOUR-APP/school` (log in with your email and password). **Progress** shows, for each class, how many children have joined and how many played today.
5. In **Teachers** you can turn a teacher off, make another teacher an admin, or tap **Reset password** to get a link for a teacher who forgot theirs (send it on WhatsApp). In **Classes** you can change a class's teacher. In **Settings** you get the school's student link, a QR poster and recent activity.

Lost your setup link or password? Ask WordWise for a new setup link.

## Teachers

1. Open the **teacher link** from your principal (`https://YOUR-APP/t/ABCDEFGH`). Or open `https://YOUR-APP/teacher/join` and type the school code `ABCD-EFGH`.
2. Type your **name, email and a password**. Tap **Join school**. You are in.
3. **Create your class:** tap **Create your class**, pick the standard (for example 5) and division (for example B), tap **Create class**.
4. **Add your students:** paste your class list (one child per line, like `1, Riya` or `2 Aarav`, straight from WhatsApp, Excel or Google Sheets) or upload a CSV file. Only first names are kept. Check the preview, tick "Parents have given consent", tap **Save**.
5. **Share:** you land on the Share screen. Tap **Print login cards** and give each child their card (it has the name, a 4-digit PIN and a QR code). Tap **Send on WhatsApp** to send parents the class link: `https://YOUR-APP/j/K7Q2MX` (your own class code).
6. Next day open `https://YOUR-APP/teacher` to see who joined and who played. If a child forgets the PIN, open **Roster** and tap **Reset PIN**, then give them the new PIN.

Forgot your password? Ask your principal for a reset link (`https://YOUR-APP/r/ABCDEFGH`).

## Students (with a parent)

1. A grown-up opens the **class link** on the phone: `https://YOUR-APP/j/K7Q2MX` (or scans the QR on your card). If it opens inside WhatsApp, tap the three dots and choose **Open in Chrome**.
2. Tap **your name**.
3. Type the **4-digit PIN** from your card on the big number pad. Forgot it? Tap "Forgot PIN" and ask your teacher.
4. Watch the short welcome (30 seconds), then tap **Let's play**. Listen, spell, learn the meaning, write a sentence. 5 words a day.
5. **Next time** it is one tap: open the app and tap **Play**. If two children share the phone, tap your own face on "Who's playing?". The app can be added to the home screen so it opens like any other app.

No class link? Open `https://YOUR-APP`, tap **I'm a Student**, type the class code from your card.
